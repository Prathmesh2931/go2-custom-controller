Step 1: The Hip Joint (Y-Z Plane)
The Hip Motor physically offsets the leg by L1. We solve the right triangle to find the effective leg length.
  • r2d = &radq;(y² + z²) : Hypotenuse to Foot.
  • L2_proj = &radq;(r2d² - L1²) : Virtual leg length.
  • θ_hip = atan2(y, -z) - sign * atan2(L1, L2_proj)

Step 2: The Calf Joint (Law of Cosines)
We now solve the 2D Tilted Leg using L2_proj as the new Vertical axis.
  • leg_dist = &radq;(x² + L2_proj²)
  • cos_calf = (leg_dist² - L2² - L3²) / (2*L2*L3)
  • θ_calf = -acos(cos_calf) (Negative = bend backwards)

Step 3: The Thigh Joint (X-Z Plane)
The thigh angle is determined by subtracting the foot's angle from the internal triangle angle.
  • α = atan2(x, L2_proj) : Foot angle from vertical.
  • β = acos(...) : Inner triangle angle via Cosine rule.
  • θ_thigh = β - α
